About us

Building systems from the ground up.

Bottom Up Impact is a food systems platform building the systems behind a stronger food economy.

A platform, not a single service.

We work across food-system infrastructure, clean energy, digital connectivity, value chains, finance and stakeholder partnerships to develop and strengthen practical opportunities within local food systems.

Our role is to bring together the people, resources, capabilities and relationships required to move an opportunity from the ground to a functioning system.

Why "Bottom Up"?

Start from the bottom. Build upward.

The Bottom Up approach starts with the realities of a place; its people, resources, needs, infrastructure, opportunities, markets and existing capabilities.

Rather than beginning with a predetermined solution, we seek to understand:

  • What exists?
  • What is needed?
  • What opportunity is present?
  • What is preventing that opportunity from working?
  • Who and what needs to be connected?

Then we connect the appropriate stakeholders, infrastructure, technology, finance, knowledge and markets.
