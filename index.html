The core idea

Stronger food systems need stronger connections.

Food-system challenges rarely exist in isolation. Reliable production needs infrastructure. Infrastructure needs energy and investment. Producers need access to markets. Markets need reliable supply. Communities need access to information, technology and opportunities.

Bottom Up Impact works across these connections, bringing stakeholders and resources together to develop practical systems that can work on the ground.

Our approach

Start from the bottom. Build upward.

We begin with the realities of a place; its people, resources, needs, opportunities and existing systems. From there, we identify what is missing, what can be strengthened and what needs to connect.

Then we bring together the appropriate stakeholders, infrastructure, finance, technology, knowledge and market relationships to build a system around the opportunity.
