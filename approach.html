07

Implement, Learn & Strengthen

Our role does not necessarily end at project design. Projects are implemented, monitored and refined.

  • Understanding what works
  • Identifying what needs to change
  • Strengthening operating models
  • Measuring results
  • Improving future interventions
08

Build for Replication and Scale

Scaling is not simply making a project bigger.

It means understanding what made the model work, what conditions are required, and what can be adapted to another context.

The Bottom Up cycle

A system that keeps learning.

Each project feeds what we learn back into the next. Bottom Up Impact continuously learns, strengthens and improves the models it builds.
